# Sample Q&A — Groww Mutual Fund FAQ Assistant

Facts-only chatbot responses for HDFC Mutual Fund schemes. Each factual answer includes exactly one source link and a "Last updated from sources" note. Out-of-scope or unsafe queries are refused per the safety rules (no advice, no comparisons/rankings, no PII).

### 1. What is the exit load for HDFC Small Cap Fund?

The HDFC Small Cap Fund charges an exit load of 1.00% of the NAV if units are redeemed or switched out within 1 year of allotment; no exit load applies after 1 year.

**Source:** https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-small-cap-fund/direct
**Last updated from sources:** July 2026

### 2. What is the riskometer for HDFC Flexi Cap Fund?

The riskometer for the HDFC Flexi Cap Fund is "Very High."

**Source:** https://www.hdfcfund.com/statutory-disclosure/riskometers
**Last updated from sources:** March 2026

### 3. What is the minimum SIP amount for HDFC Flexi Cap Fund?

The minimum amount for a regular Systematic Investment Plan (daily, weekly, or monthly SIP) in the HDFC Flexi Cap Fund is Rs 100.

**Source:** https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-flexi-cap-fund/direct
**Last updated from sources:** recently updated

### 4. What is the Total Expense Ratio (TER) of HDFC Flexi Cap Fund under the Direct Plan?

The Total Expense Ratio (TER) of the HDFC Flexi Cap Fund under the Direct Plan is 0.77% (as of 23-Aug-2026).

**Source:** https://www.hdfcfund.com/statutory-disclosure/total-expense-ratio-of-mutual-fund-schemes/reports
**Last updated from sources:** August 2026

### 5. How do I download a capital-gains statement?

You can obtain a capital-gains statement by requesting a Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) through CAMS or NSDL and selecting the "capital gains" format when generating the CAS; the CAS includes capital-gains details for all mutual fund holdings linked to your PAN.

**Source:** https://www.hdfcfund.com/learn/blog/how-get-capital-gain-statement-mutual-fund-schemes-india
**Last updated from sources:** August 2026

---

### 6. How can I redeem HDFC ELSS Tax Saver Fund units after the applicable lock-in?

After the statutory three-year lock-in period, you may redeem HDFC ELSS Tax Saver units in multiples of Rs 100 (with further redemption possible in increments of Rs 1). There is no minimum redemption requirement for unit-based redemptions. Redemption can be done through the same channels used for purchase, such as the fund's website, investor service centres, or distributors.

**Source:** https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-elss-tax-saver-fund/direct
**Last updated from sources:** November 2025

### 10. What are the six risk levels shown on the SEBI Risk-o-meter?

The SEBI Risk-o-meter displays six risk levels: Low Risk, Low to Moderate Risk, Moderate Risk, Moderately High Risk, High Risk, and Very High Risk.

**Source:** https://investor.sebi.gov.in/riskometer.html
**Last updated from sources:** recently updated

### 11. Does HDFC Large and Mid Cap Fund have a lock-in period?

The HDFC Large and Mid Cap Fund does not have a lock-in period; however, an exit load of 1.00% applies if units are redeemed or switched out within 1 year of allotment, and no exit load is charged after 1 year.

**Source:** https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-large-and-mid-cap-fund/direct
**Last updated from sources:** November 2025

### 12. What is the lock-in period for an ELSS fund?

The statutory lock-in period for an ELSS fund is three years.

**Source:** [Fund Facts HDFC TaxSaver Fund July 26 (FACTSHEET)](https://www.hdfcfund.com/explore/mutual-funds/hdfc-elss-tax-saver/direct)
**Last updated from sources:** July 2026
